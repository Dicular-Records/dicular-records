            <pre class="vC7TJ allowTextSelection">Dicular Records presents: Marcelo Bergés – Big Moon Nights / Boundaries (DIC029)
Dicular Records is proud to welcome Colombian multi-instrumentalist and sound explorer Marcelo Bergés to the label with his latest two-track EP, "Big Moon Nights" and "Boundaries."

Marcelo Bergés does not simply produce electronic music; he captures a ceremony. Known for his ability to bridge ancient Andean memories with contemporary organic textures, Bergés treats the studio as a sacred space where software is merely a vessel for his live instrumentation and vocal expression.

The EP opens with "Boundaries" (120 BPM) shifts the energy into a more rhythmic territory. While maintaining the EP's organic soul, it introduces a subtle groove that invites movement, exploring the delicate line between internal healing and external connection.

Following this, "Big Moon Nights" (118 BPM), a masterful downtempo journey that mirrors the gravity of a lunar ritual. It is a track that breathes, utilizing Marcelo’s signature blend of organic percussion and haunting melodies to create a landscape of presence and reflection.

With a history of releases on prestigious labels like Buddha-Bar and Resueño, Marcelo Bergés continues to define himself as a vital voice in the organic electronic movement. This EP is a testament to his philosophy: music not as entertainment, but as a return to the self.

RELEASE DATE 17 of April 2026!</pre>
