            <p>### Dicular News Flash ###<br>
The year couldn’t have started better for us, because we can announce the new top artist &amp; friend  Massimo Santucci at dicular. </p>

<p>He is not only musically an enrichment with his releases on well-known labels like Armada Music / Spinnin’ Records / Baroque Records / Beatfreak Recordings / Bonzai Records, he is also a great guy on a human level. He will soon spoil us with his typical melodic techno / deep house beats.<br>
Some of us toured in Europe 20 years ago with massimo and you couldn’t have asked for a better musician and colleague on the tour.</p>
<p>So great to have him with our network of musician friends.</p>
