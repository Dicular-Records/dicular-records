<div dir="auto">DIC031</div>
<div dir="auto">Dicular Records mastermind Tomtron takes the reins with his latest floor-filler, “You Are My Drug.” As the label head, Tomtron perfectly embodies the Dicular philosophy: high-energy, uncompromising, and sonically sophisticated.</div>
</div>
<div class="x14z9mp xat24cr x1lziwak x1vvkbs xtlvy1s x126k92a">
<div dir="auto">The track is a relentless powerhouse, driven by a thunderous kick and a frantic, acid-laced bassline that screams peak-time euphoria. At its heart, a hypnotic, repeating vocal hook acts as the central addiction, pulling the listener deeper into a dark, synthetic trance. With its sharp synth leads and classic rave elements, “You Are My Drug” is a sonic manifestation of obsession and intensity. It showcases Tomtron’s veteran expertise in crafting tracks that are both technically precise and raw in emotion.</div>
</div>
<div class="x14z9mp xat24cr x1lziwak x1vvkbs xtlvy1s x126k92a">
