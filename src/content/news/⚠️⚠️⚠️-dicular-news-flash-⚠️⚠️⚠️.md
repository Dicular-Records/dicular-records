            <p>We are very happy and proud to announce that none other than Mile Diskovic has joined our Dicular roster.!!</p>
<p>Mile is a passionate producer, DJ and sound engineer from Zurich, Switzerland. As a child he owned a Yamaha PortaSound PSS-470 with a synthesizer section. Since then electronic music has captivated him, and he didn’t lose his childlike enthusiasm for it to this day.</p>
<p>Inspired by life, love, humor and many great artists such as Kraftwerk, David Bowie, Balkan Sounds, Der Dritte Raum, John Holt and The Doors, he sets unmistakable musical trademarks. His music can best be described as “Echogroove” and is based in Deep House and Minimal Techno. His releases preferably contains a dash of humor that puts a smile onto the faces on every dancefloor.</p>
<p>Numerous releases by Mile have been released on Antarctic Records, Evosonic Records and Dicular Records.</p>
<p>We are really glad that he joined us.</p>
<p>Please make him an extra warm welcome.!</p>
