<div>I moved to Germany in 1986 from the UK and started to go to my first Techno parties in Cologne – Club Warehouse, Frankfurt – Dorian Gray, Omen and XS.&nbsp; In the year 1992 I went to Goa – India.&nbsp; After experiencing the free parties I returned from Goa with a totally new outlook on electronic music.</div>
<div></div>
<div>After networking with various artists and people in Germany, the UK and Holland, I started to put on parties in Saurland, Germany – the first party being ‘LSD’ (Lunatic Sky Dance).&nbsp; From then on the scene started to become established in Saurland through clubs – The Grube, The Cult, and Tara.&nbsp; I also promoted labels Blue Room Released and Atomic i.e. label parties in Germany.&nbsp; I then started to get invited to play at other parties in Germany, Europe and World-wide.</div>
<div></div>
<div>In 2000 I moved back to the UK.&nbsp; I took a step back from the scene&nbsp; my daughter was born – and I stopped playing as a DJ.</div>
<div></div>
<div>In 2020 I was unexpectedly invited to play a Retro Goa set at ZNA Festival – Portugal, which I did, and enjoyed immensely.&nbsp; This reignited my enthusiasm for DJing again – Techno, Tech House and occasionally a Retro-Goa set.</div>
