<p>DJ Since: 1998</p>
<p>Style: Progressive, Trance, Lounge, Minimal, Chillout, Electro</p>
<p>After a long time as an electronic music enthusiast, I founded a small club called Mental Blow in 1996 with some friends in the Halle-luja in Oedt. For three and a half years we hosted psychedelic trance parties (Goa parties) there with 500-700 guests. It wasn’t long before I started DJing and touring the Ruhr area under the name DJ Tassilo. In 1998 I began DJing full-time, was a resident in various clubs and had my first gigs abroad, for example in Seville, Egypt, and other places. During my career up until 2002 I founded another club in Moers and the Freundeskreis Festival. In 2004 I felt the need to try new things. I founded the Aqualounge Project with a few friends, with which I released eight chill-out films under a Creative Commons License by 2005. Aqualounge reached well over a million downloads in its second year and was broadcast in Australia, Canada, the USA, Egypt, and many other countries. Today, I play ambient, lounge, trance, house, and electronic music. Since 2021, I’ve been running the Dicular Records label with Tomtron and some friends.</p>
