                                    <p data-start="56" data-end="385">The project <strong data-start="68" data-end="81">Feinstaub</strong> consists of long-time friends Massimo Santucci and Tomtron.</p>
<p data-start="56" data-end="385">In the late 1990s, both were active together as DJs and producers in the trance scene and were fortunate to travel to many countries side by side.</p>
<p data-start="56" data-end="385">During this time, they got to know and appreciate each other both personally and artistically.</p>
<p data-start="56" data-end="385">

<p data-start="392" data-end="756">Both artists began their musical journeys before discovering techno. Massimo started out as a drummer, while Tom has been playing acoustic guitar since the age of 16.</p>
<p data-start="392" data-end="756">These roots, along with their wide-ranging musical influences, are now reflected in their joint project Feinstaub, where the duo brings an organic and groove-driven sensibility to electronic music</p>
