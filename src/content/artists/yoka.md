<div dir="ltr"><span data-olk-copy-source="MessageBody">Yoka was born in Hamburg in 1975, where he gained his first DJ</span><br>
experiences in the rap and Britcore scenes in the early 90s. Cuts and<br>
backspins with two Technics MK2 turntables were on the agenda, along<br>
with his initial stage experiences with the school band. In the summer<br>
of 1995, a transformative experience occurred when, as often happens<br>
in the Goa scene, he found himself on his first Goa party by chance<br>
and through detours. Instead of attending a Drum &amp; Bass festival, he<br>
ended up at Voov. and it became a “point of no return” for him. From<br>
then on, Psytrance captivated him entirely. He had finally found<br>
something that he couldn’t find in various other scenes, something<br>
that resonated with his own personality the most.This led to influential event visits, such as the first Shiva Moon,<br>
Pooja , opening party @ Gaswerk, or Antaro’s birthday at Delirium<br>
right next door. However, as a bass fetishist that he had always been,<br>
Goa music quickly took a back seat.On a sunday in 1995 in Elmshorn near Hamburg @ Traumraum Yoka first<br>
heard the album “Wellenbad” by 3.Raum—a revelation, a great album<br>
heard on the best soundsystem till today. and the next point of no<br>
return. ! Finally, an alternative to Astral Projection and others.<p></p>
<p>In 2002, he moved to the Cologne area to organize his own parties. In<br>
2003, he founded his own label, Synphonie Records, and organized the<br>
first “FrühlingsSynphonie Festival” in Kierspe that same year, later<br>
moving it to Wasserburg Geretzhoven. In 2006, the debut album<br>
“Butterfly Effect” was released under Synphonetic with DJ Quantec. In<br>
2011, the temporarily final album by Native Radio, “Macula,” was<br>
released, along with several releases on Plusquam Rec., Prog-On<br>
Syndicate, and Planet Ben Rec.</p>
<p>Over the past decades, Yoka has been active for labels such as Savva<br>
Rec., Mental-Arts Rec., and Tesseract Studio Serbia. Additionally, he<br>
was involved with Café3Klang in Essen during its last three years,<br>
responsible for the lineup, flyer design, and bookings, while also<br>
serving as a resident DJ. It was during this time that Yoka developed<br>
a love for the Ruhr area.</p>
<p>Afterward, a creative break was necessary, which lasted for five<br>
years, allowing him to rediscover his musical orientation. And now we<br>
finally come to the crucial topics related to music. In progressive<br>
Psytrance, there is hardly anything truly progressive in the<br>
conventional sense. There are few elements that build upon each other,<br>
and the artists in this scene seemingly have a different purpose in<br>
mind than what I fell in love with almost 30 years ago. For me, one of<br>
the main purposes of psychedelic music back then was to achieve a<br>
trance-like state of mind through the music and dance, comparable to<br>
African drum groups or the Sufi dervishes. All of this should be done<br>
in synchronization with everyone else in the room, creating a<br>
synchronized mass on the dancefloor, with the kick drum filling the<br>
space, almost tangible, and a shift in the perception of time. The DJ<br>
should not distract participants from their concentration with<br>
conspicuous, highly noticeable sounds or sudden music changes.<br>
Ideally, the listener should be unaware of how the elements of a track<br>
evolve and dissolve over the course of hours.</p>
<p>I don’t want to be the type who claims that “everything was better in<br>
the past,” but it was different, and the purpose has indeed changed<br>
today. And the musical content reflects these changes, that’s why it<br>
was time to leave the Psytrance scene, we simply grew apart,<br>
darling… However, some of the necessary elements can still be found<br>
in Ethno, Techno and the countless genres, even though a touch of<br>
Psychedelic will always remain. The sound generally ranges between 125<br>
and 135 BPM and should ideally have playtimes of 3 to 5 hours, as<br>
stories need to be told in peace. Not too fast, not too slow…</p>
<p>Since early 2023, Yoka and Kachina have formed the DJ team “YoKachina”<br>
– stay tuned for upcoming dates…</p>
