<p>Oli Inkognito is one half of the famous Dj/Producer Team s.h.e.e.p. (with Marc van Mumpitz). From 2002 to 2007 he was acting as A&amp;R &amp; Label Dj for the Munich based Label Hardboiled Records and from 2007 to 2010 he was playing as a Label Dj for the well known German progressive Label Vertikal Records.</p>
<p>In 1998 he started djing at small parties – now he can look back on uncountable parties and festivals all over Germany, Europe and abroad. Some specials like AfrikaBurn (South Afrika), Natraj Temple Partys (Munich), Tresor (Berlin), Klang der Essenzen, Moonchild, XYMOX, La Pura Vida @ Castillo del Mar (La Gomera, Spain), Soma Solaris, Nine Bar (Vagator/Goa), Zoo thousand and two (South Africa), Mandorla (South Africa) Darklines (Austria), Blue Butterfly (Switzerland), Zirk Partys (Amsterdam), D-Apontia Gathering (Greece), Full Moon &amp; Half Moon Partys (Thailand) Vortex and Tribal Vision Records Label Party (Czech Republic) Gathering of the Tribes, Electricity Festival, Organic Atmosphere and Shuria’s World (Berlin) are just a few of them.</p>
<p>He played as resident Dj at GOTEC over 7 years. His style is Organic Techno with a wide range, deep grooving and storytelling, always focused on the crowd. He also loves the alternative floors and is proven to turn a dancefloor mad with his set. He was the Organizer of the sucessful “PINKE PUNKTE – progressive Grooves” Partys every 2nd month in Karlsruhe and from time to time in Munich (Natraj Temple / Traumraum Club and Substation). Different radio shows like “Stripping Sounds” on&nbsp;danceradio.gr&nbsp;, Radio Wüste Welle, Hardboiled Radio, or Radio Free Tankwa (AfrikaBurn) supported him a lot over the last years.</p>
<p>Together with Dj Larimar and Marc van Mumpitz he founded the legendary Spiralclan in the year 2000 and they also organized their own parties around Munich. (Psychedelic Afternoon Afterhours, Pleasure Addicted Partys, Buddha Bar, Rossdance and of course Groove Garden and the Allach Partys 😉 the family knows!)</p>
<p>The first track from s.h.e.e.p. has been released on their own Hardboiled Wonderland Compilation and on Yellow Sunshine Explosion. They also made a remix from POTS – Dandy Division for Tribal Vision Records.</p>
<p>Oli Inkognito´s focus is actually on Djing!</p>
