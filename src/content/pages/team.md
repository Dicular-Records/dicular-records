<h3><span style="font-size: 18pt;"><strong>Tomtron</strong></span></h3>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt; color: #ffffff;"><strong>Label Head and founder of Dicular Records</strong></span></p>
<p><img data-recalc-dims="1" fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-3763" src="/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/IMG_E1193.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="225" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/IMG_E1193.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/IMG_E1193.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/IMG_E1193.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/IMG_E1193.jpg 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/IMG_E1193.jpg 2048w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/IMG_E1193.jpg 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/IMG_E1193.jpg 1800w" sizes="(max-width: 300px) 100vw, 300px"></p>
<p>Early 1996, Tomtron began playing as a DJ in the house and techno scene. As a guest at the first psychedelic trance festival, he fell in love with this highly emotional music with an oriental touch. Not long after, he begins spinning psychedelic trance music as a DJ, performing gigs in Israel, England, Croatia, Denmark, Switzerland, France, and all across Germany.</p>
<p>In his role as an A&amp;R at Nova Tekk distribution, he later put together numerous compilations of Goa Head, Tantrance, Pulse, and Goa Party tracks. During this time, he visits a new artist called Timm Liesegang to sign some music for Novatekk. This was the best thing that could have happened to him in this time because a long and great friendship was formed and Timm Liesgang and Tomtron started making music together, releasing two albums, and playing a lot live together.</p>
<p>At the same time Tomtron starts organizing in Oberhausen Germany the Indoor / Outdoor event “Klang Der Essenzen” with a great team and line-up each two months, which continues to run for over 6 years.</p>
<p>Therefore, it was only a matter of time before he started his own record label, Vertical Records. By this time, as a DJ and producer, his style had already evolved to be more UK Trance and progressive.</p>
<p>After a few years of running the label, Tomtron decided to take a break from music – focusing his time and energy on his actual career.</p>
<p>In 2021, nearly 12 years later, Tomtron finally picked up producing again, resulting some single releases. After starting his new label Dicular Records at the end of 2021, he also organized the first “Klang Der Essenzen” event in the beginning of 2022 – and even started DJing again soon after.</p>

<h3><span style="font-size: 18pt;"><strong>Massimo Santucci</strong></span></h3>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt; color: #ffffff;"><strong>Distribution/Production/Mastering</strong></span></p>
<p><img data-recalc-dims="1" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-4315" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/99e2374b-06da-495d-ba11-0e02fa671e7c.jpg" alt="" width="225" height="300" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/99e2374b-06da-495d-ba11-0e02fa671e7c.jpg 225w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/04/99e2374b-06da-495d-ba11-0e02fa671e7c.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/04/99e2374b-06da-495d-ba11-0e02fa671e7c.jpg 1152w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/04/99e2374b-06da-495d-ba11-0e02fa671e7c.jpg 1536w" sizes="(max-width: 225px) 100vw, 225px"></p>
<p>Armada Music / Spinnin’ Records / Baroque Records / Beatfreak Recordings / Bonzai Records</p>
<p>Massimo Santucci is a musicians and music-producer from the south-Italian mediterranean area that, since the beginning of the 80’s, passionately plays with music. Starting with self-made drums of bucked and pots, he had developed -mid of the 90’s- to a serious Jazz-Rock drummer.</p>
<p>1996 he discovered his passion for electronic music when he launched his very first Electronic Music project Analogue Dream (Psy-Trance) whereby, in the course of 4 years, he completed hundert’s of live performances around Germany and Europe. After a few compilation releases on Shiva Space Technology, Nova Tekk or Nephilim Records, 2002 Massimo released he first full-length album “Massimo Santucci – Al Dente”, on Liquid Audio Soundz. In the same year came also the progressive-trance album of Prototyp, a cooperation with Linus Wessel (Tarsis) and Panajotis Tzanoglou on the German Insolation label. With all this project’s, Massimo toured constantly through Germany, many European cities and partly some overseas gigs between the years 2000 and 2004.</p>
<p>In 2004 he took the challenge to work as music-producer and sound-engineer for the record company OpaqueMusic that, at that time, was responsible for many of the club hits and managed some of the aspiring DJ’s such as Kurd Maverick, Eddie Thoneick, Tube &amp; Berger, Juliet Sikora a.m.m.. Many of the productions like The Mashup Kids – Won’t let you Down, Kurd Maverick – The Rub a.m.m., has been licensed on majors such as Universal Music, EMI, Kontor Records, Superstar Recordings while some others climbed till on top #1 of the UK (LMC vs. U2 – Take me to the Clouds Above) or DDC Charts (Eddie Thonheick &amp; Kurd Maverick – Love Sensation).</p>
<p>Parallel to his work as co-producer and mastering engineer, Massimo has released many of his own production of this different project’s Massimo Santucci, Maxrage, Jackus &amp; Santucci or Tribalism on well-know Labes such as Armin van Buuren’s Armada Music, Ferry Corsten’s Flashover, Robbie Rivera’s Juicy Music a.m.m.. His tracks find place in the set’s or radio shows of biggies like Paul van Dyk, Sander van Doorn, Moguai, Roger Sanchez, Bob Sinclair, Tom Novy a.m.m.. Massimo’s remixes of Racheal Starr’s “Crash” (2006) or Sergio D’Angelo &amp; Andrea Love “Can’t Live Without Your Groove”, were broadcasted on heavy rotation on some important radios incl. BBC Radio 1 Essential Mix, 1Live, Radio Deejay, Radio RTL 105. Crash was 6 weeks in the US Billboard Dance Charts. The track „String of Cesar“ released on Ferry Corsten’s Label, was licensed for Moguai’s Compilation Punx up The Volume on Embassy One, the German Label of Ministry of Sound.</p>
<p>In 2011, Massimo got the great pleasure to work together with one of his idols, the Italian jazz percussionist Tony Esposito, on a remake of his 80s hit Kalimba de Luna. The maxi single came as “Kalimba” on the Dutch dance giant Spinnin’ Records. In 2012, under the project of Maxrage, the album “Powder” came out on Tribal Vision Records, which was the result from a collaboration with another gifted music producer Peter Gun aka Peter Jürgens, which was immediately followed by a small Mexico tour.</p>
<p>In the same time he startet a group of Labels to release all the tracks he produced in the last months. The debut Release Flowquency on the same named label was played over a few weeks on 1Live Radio by Moguai and Licensed on the Compilation MTV Mobile Beats presented during the the same named Festival in Cologne Germany. Recently he had a small comeback with his 1st project Analogue Dream releasing around 10 Tracks and an album that include a bunch of 1997 productions on DAT Tape. Analogue Dream absolved a dozen of Live Acts but Massimo is going now back to his Studio to bring some modern melodic Techno &amp; House tunes in the very near future.</p>

<p><span style="font-size: 18pt;"><strong>Oli Inkognito</strong></span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt; color: #ffffff;"><strong>Artist &amp; Repertoire / Networking / Production</strong></span></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><img data-recalc-dims="1" loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-3716" src="/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Oli-Inkognito.jpg" alt="" width="193" height="300" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Oli-Inkognito.jpg 193w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Oli-Inkognito.jpg 385w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 193px) 100vw, 193px"></span></strong></p>
<p>Oli Inkognito is one half of the famous Dj/Producer Team s.h.e.e.p. (with Marc van Mumpitz). From 2002 to 2007 he was acting as A&amp;R &amp; Label Dj for the Munich based Label Hardboiled Records and from 2007 to 2010 he was playing as a Label Dj for the well known German progressive Label Vertikal Records.</p>
<p>In 1998 he started djing at small parties – now he can look back on uncountable parties and festivals all over Germany, Europe and abroad. Some specials like AfrikaBurn (South Afrika), Natraj Temple Partys (Munich), Tresor (Berlin), Klang der Essenzen, Moonchild, XYMOX, La Pura Vida @ Castillo del Mar (La Gomera, Spain), Soma Solaris, Nine Bar (Vagator/Goa), Zoo thousand and two (South Africa), Mandorla (South Africa) Darklines (Austria), Blue Butterfly (Switzerland), Zirk Partys (Amsterdam), D-Apontia Gathering (Greece), Full Moon &amp; Half Moon Partys (Thailand) Vortex and Tribal Vision Records Label Party (Czech Republic) Gathering of the Tribes, Electricity Festival, Organic Atmosphere and Shuria’s World (Berlin) are just a few of them.</p>
<p>He played as resident Dj at GOTEC over 7 years. His style is Organic Techno with a wide range, deep grooving and storytelling, always focused on the crowd. He also loves the alternative floors and is proven to turn a dancefloor mad with his set. He was the Organizer of the sucessful “PINKE PUNKTE – progressive Grooves” Partys every 2nd month in Karlsruhe and from time to time in Munich (Natraj Temple / Traumraum Club and Substation). Different radio shows like “Stripping Sounds” on&nbsp;danceradio.gr&nbsp;, Radio Wüste Welle, Hardboiled Radio, or Radio Free Tankwa (AfrikaBurn) supported him a lot over the last years.</p>
<p>Together with Dj Larimar and Marc van Mumpitz he founded the legendary Spiralclan in the year 2000 and they also organized their own parties around Munich. (Psychedelic Afternoon Afterhours, Pleasure Addicted Partys, Buddha Bar, Rossdance and of course Groove Garden and the Allach Partys 😉 the family knows!)</p>
<p>The first track from s.h.e.e.p. has been released on their own Hardboiled Wonderland Compilation and on Yellow Sunshine Explosion. They also made a remix from POTS – Dandy Division for Tribal Vision Records.</p>
<p>Oli Inkognito´s focus is actually on Djing!</p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18pt;"><strong>Mile Diskovic</strong></span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt; color: #ffffff;"><strong>Production / Promotion</strong></span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong><img data-recalc-dims="1" loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-4338" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Mile_Diskovic_wide.jpg" alt="" width="300" height="200" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Mile_Diskovic_wide-scaled.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Mile_Diskovic_wide-scaled.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Mile_Diskovic_wide-scaled.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Mile_Diskovic_wide-scaled.jpg 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Mile_Diskovic_wide-scaled.jpg 2048w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Mile_Diskovic_wide-scaled.jpg 1800w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 300px) 100vw, 300px"></strong></span></p>
<p>Since 1999 his career has been very diverse. It has taken him through numerous music and film sound productions.</p>
<p>He has performed as a DJ and played at Hip Hop, Punk and Piano concerts.</p>
<p>From 2010 to 2013 he deepened his knowledge by studying at the Audio Engineering School SAE, which took his technical skills to a new level.</p>
<p>Mile’s music is deeply rooted in the genres of Techno, Disco, Indie Dance, Funk, Pop, Deep House, Minimal Techno and experimental sounds of all kinds, which he enriches with his unique style that he calls “echo groove”.</p>
<p>His tracks are inspired by a variety of musical influences, including Psychedelic Rock, Disco, Funk, balkan sounds and classical composers such as Händel. Artists such as Der Dritte Raum, Jiggler, Alex Union, The Doors,</p>
<p>Damon Jee and Rick James shape his sound as much as nature itself, which he sees as an inexhaustible source of inspiration.</p>
<p>Since 2017 Mile has released a number of singles and EPs.</p>
<p>Since 2018 he’s hosting the radio show “Galaxyspacenight” on Radio Stadtfilter Winterthur and Radio Bern in Switzerland.</p>
<p>He also uses this platform to share his musical vision and take his listeners into the depths of echo and groove.</p>
<p>Miles’ sound is characterized by a playful lightness and a pinch of humor that gets every dance floor moving and puts a smile on the dancers’ faces.</p>
<p>He always remains true to his creative curiosity and strives to continuously expand the boundaries of electronic music without losing sight of the roots of his inspiration.</p>
<p>So far his music has been released on Antarctic Records, Roo Reords, Evosonic Records and Dicular Records.</p>
