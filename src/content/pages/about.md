<p style="text-align: center;">Dicular Records, Music label, Artist Network, Production &amp; Events!</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">Dicular Records is focused on progressive house, melodic techno &amp; progressive trance, founded by DJ Tomtron in 2021.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">The commitment to music based intention result of well experienced Team deeply rooted in nearly all kind of electronic dance Music.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">Dicular Records is releasing more and more fresh and outstanding tunes.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">Dicular Records has already grown a remarkable catalogue of unique and diverse releases in a short period of time since its inception.</p>
<p style="text-align: center;">We support young artists in production and accompany them until their first own release. We repeatedly organize small independent events where the artists can perform their works.</p>
